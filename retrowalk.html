RetroWalk

Three.js HTML5 CSS3 JavaScript

For our finals project, we built RetroWalk, inspired by Pixar's Inside Out. It is a browser-based 3D museum where each of my circle of friends has their own Memory Hall, also as a way to preserve and celebrate the memories of my circle of friend group, NYERK. Each memory hall has a glowing corridor filled with orbs that hold their photos and videos, just like the memory shelves from the movie. You walk through in first-person, step into someone's hall, and experience the moments they wanted to share.

The idea

Photos and videos buried in chat threads fade away. RetroWalk gives them a place to live: a walkable 3D museum where each friend has a Memory Hall, and every memory inside glows as an orb you can open and relive.
